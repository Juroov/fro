## About / Founder Story

FRO Solar Power Installation and Services is an installer and integrator of
solar energy and power generation systems, with 28 years of industry
expertise and a strong track record in power projects, particularly in the
Middle East.

The founder brings 17 years of field experience in the Middle East as a
Project Manager, directly involved in installation, testing, commissioning,
operation, and maintenance of power generation facilities in:

- Iraq
- Kurdistan Region
- Afghanistan
- Lebanon
- Kingdom of Saudi Arabia (KSA)
- Israel
- Jordan

He has hands-on experience commissioning power plants up to 175 MW, across
gas, diesel, HFO (Heavy Fuel Oil), and solar technologies.

Today FRO Solar brings that international experience to the Philippine
market: solar power systems, hybrid energy systems, grid-tied systems,
battery energy storage, and power-generation projects — for residential,
commercial, and industrial clients in and around Davao del Sur.
